About Me

From equations to algorithms

My path into data started with Mathematics — a B.Sc. and Master's degree that built a foundation in logical reasoning, statistics, and structured problem-solving. Five years of teaching Mathematics sharpened my ability to break down complex ideas and explain them clearly, a skill that now shows up directly in how I communicate data insights to non-technical stakeholders.

Today I work as a Data Scientist & Data Analyst, applying that same analytical mindset to real datasets — cleaning and exploring data, building statistical models, designing Power BI dashboards, and experimenting with Machine Learning and AI to uncover patterns that support better decisions.

I'm particularly interested in the space where statistics, machine learning, and business intelligence meet — where a well-modeled dataset can turn into a genuinely useful, decision-ready insight.
